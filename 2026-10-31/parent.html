Q1 · 比较效率

小明 6 小时完成了 2160 个零件，李师傅 8 小时完成了 2560 个零件。谁的工作效率更高？高出多少？

  1. 李师傅更高，每小时多 40 个
  2. 小明更高，每小时多 40 个 ✔
  3. 两人一样快
  4. 小明更高，每小时多 640 个

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5

小明的效率：2160 ÷ 6 = 360（个/小时）。李师傅的效率：2560 ÷ 8 = 320（个/小时）。360 − 320 = 40，所以小明更高，每小时多 40 个。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 算反了。小明 360 个/小时，李师傅 320 个/小时，是小明更高。
C: 两人效率不同：360 ≠ 320。
D: 640 是两人完成量的差（2560−2160），不是每小时效率的差。应先各自算出每小时再比较：360−320=40。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 用总工作量差代替效率差；谁快搞反。

Q2 · Find the work (r×t=W)

Lucas packs 185 cartons per hour. How many cartons does he pack in 12 hours?

  1. 197 cartons
  2. 2220 cartons ✔
  3. 173 cartons
  4. 1850 cartons

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5

Work = rate × time = 185 × 12. 185 × 10 = 1850 and 185 × 2 = 370, so 1850 + 370 = 2220 cartons.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You added 185 + 12. Multiply rate by time: 185 × 12 = 2220.
C: You subtracted 185 − 12. Work is rate times time.
D: 1850 is only 185 × 10. He worked 12 hours, so add two more hours: 1850 + 370 = 2220.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Adding or subtracting instead of multiplying; multiplying by 10 only.

Q3 · 多车多趟求一车一趟

3 辆卡车一共运了 2 趟，共运走 3750 箱水果。照这样算，1 辆卡车运 1 趟可以运多少箱？

  1. 1250 箱
  2. 1875 箱
  3. 625 箱 ✔
  4. 7500 箱

Answer 答案: C · Level 難度 5

3 辆卡车运 2 趟共 3750 箱。先 ÷3 再 ÷2（或先 ÷2 再 ÷3）：3750 ÷ 3 = 1250，1250 ÷ 2 = 625。所以 1 辆卡车 1 趟运 625 箱。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 1250 只除了卡车数（3750÷3），还要再除趟数 2。
B: 1875 只除了趟数（3750÷2），还要再除卡车数 3。
D: 7500 是乘了 2，不是除。要把总箱数除以卡车数和趟数。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 只除一项（车或趟）；误乘。

Q4 · Order with brackets

Compute: [960 ÷ (16 − 4)] × 25. Which answer is correct?

  1. 1500
  2. 2000 ✔
  3. 60
  4. 24000

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

Inside ( ) first: 16 − 4 = 12. Then inside [ ]: 960 ÷ 12 = 80. Then × 25: 80 × 25 = 2000. (Smart way: 80 × 100 ÷ 4 = 8000 ÷ 4 = 2000.)

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You may have done 960 ÷ 16 = 60, then 60 − 4 = 56, then something else. Do (16−4) first, then divide 960 by 12.
C: 60 is 960 ÷ 16. You skipped the (16−4) step. Use 960 ÷ 12 = 80, then 80 × 25 = 2000.
D: You multiplied 960 × 25 first. Follow brackets: [960 ÷ 12] × 25 = 80 × 25 = 2000.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Ignoring brackets; multiplying before dividing.

Q5 · 结合律简便运算

用简便方法计算 125 × 48 × 8。正确结果是哪一个？

  1. 48000 ✔
  2. 6000
  3. 5000
  4. 40000

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

先算 125 × 8 = 1000，再算 1000 × 48 = 48000。所以 125 × 48 × 8 = 48000。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: 6000 是 125 × 48，还没有乘上 8。完整算法：125×8=1000，再×48=48000。
C: 可能把 48 看成 40：125×8×40=40000 也不对；正确是 ×48 得 48000。
D: 40000 像是用了 40 而不是 48（125×8×40）。要用 48：1000×48=48000。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 算完 125×48 就停；把 48 当成 40。

Q6 · Plan vs actual ⭐ Challenge 挑戰題

A bakery planned to bake 5400 loaves in 9 days (same number each day). They finished all 5400 loaves in only 6 days. How many more loaves per day did they bake than planned?

  1. 300 loaves more per day ✔
  2. 900 loaves more per day
  3. 600 loaves more per day
  4. 150 loaves more per day

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 5

Planned rate = 5400 ÷ 9 = 600 loaves per day. Actual rate = 5400 ÷ 6 = 900 loaves per day. Difference = 900 − 600 = 300 more loaves per day.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: 900 is the actual daily amount, not how many more than the plan. Subtract: 900 − 600 = 300.
C: 600 is the planned daily amount. The question asks how many more than planned: 900 − 600 = 300.
D: You may have halved 300 or used (9−6). Find both daily rates first, then subtract.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Reporting plan or actual rate instead of the difference; using day difference without rates.
