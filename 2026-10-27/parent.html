Lesson 27: Work Rate (工作效率／工程問題) · Level 3 · Shanghai G4 / Singapore P4

Work done ÷ Time = Rate (工作效率)
Rate × Time = Work done (工作量)
Work done ÷ Rate = Time (時間)
Work rate = the amount done in one unit of time (單位時間內完成的工作量)

Q1 · Find the rate

Emma baked 540 muffins in 6 hours. Lucas baked 720 muffins in 9 hours. Who works faster, and by how many muffins per hour?

  1. Lucas, by 180 muffins per hour
  2. Emma, by 10 muffins per hour ✔
  3. Lucas, by 10 muffins per hour
  4. They work at the same rate

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 3

Rate = work ÷ time. Emma: 540 ÷ 6 = 90 muffins/h. Lucas: 720 ÷ 9 = 80 muffins/h. 90 − 80 = 10, so Emma is faster by 10 muffins per hour.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You compared the totals (720 − 540 = 180). First divide each total by its time to get the rate.
C: You found the difference of 10 correctly but picked the wrong person: Emma's rate (90) is higher than Lucas's (80).
D: Check: 540 ÷ 6 = 90 and 720 ÷ 9 = 80 — they are not equal.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Comparing totals instead of rates; mixing up who is faster.

Q2 · Find the time

Mia packs 125 boxes per hour. How many hours does she need to pack 875 boxes?

  1. 750 hours
  2. 7 hours ✔
  3. 8 hours
  4. 109375 hours

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 3

Time = work ÷ rate = 875 ÷ 125 = 7 hours. Check: 125 × 7 = 875.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You subtracted (875 − 125). To find time, divide the work by the rate.
C: Place-value slip: 875 ÷ 125 = 7, not 8. Check: 125 × 8 = 1000.
D: You multiplied (875 × 125). Rate × time gives work; here divide instead.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Subtracting or multiplying instead of dividing; place-value errors.

Q3 · Find the work done

Oliver paints 168 fences per hour. He paints for 5 hours. How many fences does he paint altogether?

  1. 173 fences
  2. 840 fences ✔
  3. 33 fences
  4. 163 fences

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 3

Work = rate × time = 168 × 5 = 840 fences.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You added (168 + 5). Each hour he paints 168, so multiply: 168 × 5.
C: You roughly divided. Use work = rate × time.
D: You subtracted (168 − 5). Use work = rate × time.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Adding, subtracting, or dividing instead of multiplying.

Q4 · Remaining work

There are 960 stickers to put on cards. Ava puts on 120 stickers per hour. After she works for 3 hours, how many stickers are still left?

  1. 360 stickers
  2. 600 stickers ✔
  3. 840 stickers
  4. 240 stickers

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 3

In 3 hours Ava finishes 120 × 3 = 360 stickers. Left: 960 − 360 = 600 stickers.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 360 is the amount already done, not what is left. Remaining = 960 − 360 = 600.
C: You only subtracted one hour's work (960 − 120). She worked 3 hours: subtract 360.
D: You may have used 960 ÷ 4 or another wrong step. Done = 120 × 3 = 360; left = 600.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Reporting done amount instead of remaining; subtracting only one hour.

Q5 · Find the rate

Noah washed 448 plates in 7 hours. What is Noah's work rate in plates per hour?

  1. 64 plates per hour ✔
  2. 3136 plates per hour
  3. 441 plates per hour
  4. 455 plates per hour

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 3 · Review 複習

Rate = work ÷ time = 448 ÷ 7 = 64 plates per hour.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: You multiplied (448 × 7). Rate is work divided by time.
C: You subtracted (448 − 7). Use rate = work ÷ time.
D: You added (448 + 7). Divide the plates by the hours.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Multiplying, adding, or subtracting instead of dividing work by time.

Q6 · Working together after a head start ⭐ Challenge 挑戰題

There are 1800 pages to copy. Lucas copies 150 pages per hour alone for 4 hours. Then Mia joins and copies 250 pages per hour while Lucas keeps working at 150 pages per hour. How many more hours until they finish together?

  1. 12 hours
  2. 3 hours ✔
  3. 4.8 hours
  4. 8 hours

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 4

Lucas alone for 4 hours: 150 × 4 = 600 pages done. Left: 1800 − 600 = 1200. Together rate: 150 + 250 = 400 pages/h. Time left: 1200 ÷ 400 = 3 hours. Check: Lucas 450 + Mia 750 = 1200.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You may have divided 1200 by 100 or used a wrong combined rate. Together they do 400 per hour: 1200 ÷ 400 = 3.
C: You used only Mia's rate (1200 ÷ 250 = 4.8). Add Lucas's rate too when they work together.
D: You used only Lucas's rate for the remaining (1200 ÷ 150). Add Mia's 250 as well.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Forgetting the head-start amount; using only one person's rate for the remaining work.
