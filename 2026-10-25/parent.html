Lesson 27: Work Rate (工作效率／工程問題) · Level 2 · Shanghai G4 / Singapore P4

Work done ÷ Time = Rate (工作效率)
Rate × Time = Work done (工作量)
Work done ÷ Rate = Time (時間)
Work rate = the amount done in one unit of time (單位時間內完成的工作量)

Q1 · Find the rate

Mrs. Brown made 14 buns in 2 hours. Mrs. Smith made 18 buns in 3 hours. Who works faster, and by how many buns per hour?

  1. Mrs. Smith, by 4 buns per hour
  2. Mrs. Brown, by 1 bun per hour ✔
  3. Mrs. Smith, by 1 bun per hour
  4. They work at the same rate

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 2

Rate = work ÷ time. Mrs. Brown: 14 ÷ 2 = 7 buns/h. Mrs. Smith: 18 ÷ 3 = 6 buns/h. 7 − 6 = 1, so Mrs. Brown is faster by 1 bun per hour.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You compared the totals (18 − 14 = 4). You must first divide each total by its time to get the rate (工作效率).
C: You found the difference of 1 correctly but picked the wrong person: Mrs. Brown's rate (7) is higher than Mrs. Smith's (6).
D: Check the division: 14 ÷ 2 = 7 and 18 ÷ 3 = 6 — they are not equal.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Comparing totals instead of rates; dividing time by work.

Q2 · Find the time

Lucas reads 8 pages per hour. How many hours does he need to read a 48-page storybook?

  1. 40 hours
  2. 384 hours
  3. 6 hours ✔
  4. 56 hours

Answer 答案: C · Level 難度 2

Time = work ÷ rate = 48 ÷ 8 = 6 hours.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You subtracted (48 − 8). To find time, divide the work by the rate.
B: You multiplied (48 × 8). Rate × time gives work, but here we already know the work — divide instead.
D: You added (48 + 8). Use time = work ÷ rate.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Multiplying or subtracting instead of dividing.

Q3 · Find the work done

Emma sews 12 shirts per hour. She sews for 3 hours. How many shirts does she sew altogether?

  1. 4 shirts
  2. 36 shirts ✔
  3. 15 shirts
  4. 9 shirts

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 2

Work = rate × time = 12 × 3 = 36 shirts.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You divided (12 ÷ 3). 12 is already the amount per hour; for 3 hours multiply.
C: You added (12 + 3). Each hour she sews 12, so it is 12 + 12 + 12 = 12 × 3.
D: You subtracted (12 − 3). Use work = rate × time.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Dividing or adding instead of multiplying.

Q4 · Compare rates

Big sister takes 6 hours to finish a job alone. Little sister takes 4 hours to finish the same job alone. Which statement is correct?

  1. Big sister is faster because she works more hours
  2. They are equally fast because it is the same job
  3. Big sister does 1/4 of the job each hour
  4. Little sister is faster because she does 1/4 of the job each hour, more than 1/6 ✔

Answer 答案: D · Level 難度 2

Treat the job as 1. Big sister: 1 ÷ 6 = 1/6 per hour. Little sister: 1 ÷ 4 = 1/4 per hour. 1/4 > 1/6, so little sister is faster — same job, less time.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: More hours for the same job means slower, not faster. Rate = work ÷ time.
B: Same job, but different times, so the rates are different.
C: You mixed up the sisters: big sister takes 6 hours, so she does 1/6 each hour.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Thinking more time means faster.

Q5 · Find the time

A machine makes 120 parts per hour. How many hours does it need to make 960 parts?

  1. 8 hours ✔
  2. 840 hours
  3. 80 hours
  4. 12 hours

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 2

Time = work ÷ rate = 960 ÷ 120 = 8 hours.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: You subtracted (960 − 120). Divide the work by the rate.
C: Place-value slip: 960 ÷ 120 = 8, not 80. Check: 120 × 8 = 960.
D: You divided 960 by 80 or guessed. Check: 120 × 12 = 1440, too many.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Place-value errors when dividing by 120.

Q6 · Working together ⭐ Challenge 挑戰題

Mum cleans 8 windows per hour and Dad cleans 12 windows per hour. The house has 40 windows. If they clean together, how many hours will it take?

  1. 5 hours
  2. 10 hours
  3. 2 hours ✔
  4. 20 hours

Answer 答案: C · Level 難度 3

Together their rate is 8 + 12 = 20 windows per hour. Time = 40 ÷ 20 = 2 hours. Check: Mum 16 + Dad 24 = 40.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You used only Mum's rate (40 ÷ 8). When working together, add the rates first.
B: You subtracted the rates (12 − 8 = 4, 40 ÷ 4). Working together means adding rates.
D: 20 is their combined rate per hour, not the time. Next step: 40 ÷ 20.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Using one person's rate; subtracting rates; stopping at the combined rate.
