Lesson 27: Work Rate (工作效率／工程問題) · Level: Shanghai G4 / Singapore P4

Work done ÷ Time = Rate (工作效率)
Rate × Time = Work done (工作量)
Work done ÷ Rate = Time (時間)
Work rate = the amount done in one unit of time (單位時間內完成的工作量)

Question 1 · Find the rate

Auntie Zhang made 14 buns in 2 hours. Auntie Li made 18 buns in 3 hours. How many buns does each make per hour? Who works faster?

Answer 答案: Zhang 7 buns/h; Li 6 buns/h; Auntie Zhang is faster

Rate = work ÷ time
Zhang: 14 ÷ 2 = 7 buns per hour
Li: 18 ÷ 3 = 6 buns per hour
7 > 6, so Auntie Zhang is faster

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Comparing totals (18 > 14) instead of dividing by time; dividing time by work.

Question 2 · Find the time

Ming reads 8 pages per hour. How many hours does he need to read a 48-page storybook?

Answer 答案: 6 hours

Time = work ÷ rate
48 ÷ 8 = 6 hours

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 48 × 8 or 48 − 8.

Question 3 · Find the work done

Hua sews 12 shirts per hour. She sews for 3 hours. How many shirts does she sew altogether?

Answer 答案: 36 shirts

Work = rate × time
12 × 3 = 36 shirts

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 12 ÷ 3 = 4 or 12 + 3 = 15.

Question 4 · Compare rates

Big sister takes 6 hours to finish a job alone. Little sister takes 4 hours to finish the same job alone. Who has the higher work rate? Explain why.

Answer 答案: Little sister (1/4 job per hour > 1/6 job per hour)

Treat the whole job as 1
Big sister: 1 ÷ 6 = 1/6 job per hour
Little sister: 1 ÷ 4 = 1/4 job per hour
1/4 > 1/6 — same job in less time means higher rate

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Thinking more hours means more work, so choosing big sister.

Question 5 · Find the time

A machine makes 120 parts per hour. How many hours does it need to make 960 parts?

Answer 答案: 8 hours

Time = work ÷ rate
960 ÷ 120 = 8 hours

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Writing 120 ÷ 960, or subtracting.

Question 6 ⭐ Challenge 挑戰題

Mum cleans 8 windows per hour and Dad cleans 12 windows per hour. The house has 40 windows. If they clean together, how many hours will it take?

Answer 答案: 2 hours

Combined rate = 8 + 12 = 20 windows per hour
Time = 40 ÷ 20 = 2 hours
Check: Mum 16 + Dad 24 = 40 ✓

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Using only one person's rate (40 ÷ 8), or subtracting the rates.
