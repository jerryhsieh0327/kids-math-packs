Lesson 27 Work Rate + syllabus review (未知因数／剩余／换算天数) · Level 5 · Shanghai G4 / Singapore P4

Work ÷ Time = Rate (工作效率)
Rate × Time = Work (工作量)
Work ÷ Rate = Time (求时间／天数)
Remaining = Total − Done (剩余工作量)
Missing factor: product ÷ known factor (未知因数：用除法验算)
Plan vs actual: compare daily rates (计划与实际)

Q1 · 按效率推算天数

王阿姨 6 天缝了 2160 件衣服。照这样的速度，缝 3240 件衣服需要几天？

  1. 9 天 ✔
  2. 15 天
  3. 8 天
  4. 12 天

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 5

先求每天的效率：2160 ÷ 6 = 360（件/天）。再求缝 3240 件需要的天数：3240 ÷ 360 = 9。所以需要 9 天。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: 15 是把 2160+3240 再乱除，或把两段天数凑在一起。应先算效率 360 件/天，再 3240÷360=9。
C: 8 可能来自 3240÷（某个错误商）。正确：2160÷6=360，3240÷360=9。
D: 12 像是 2160÷6×（3240÷1080）之类的误算。正确天数是 3240÷360=9。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 未先求效率就硬凑天数；把总件数相加再除。

Q2 · Remaining work

There are 4860 bookmarks to make. Lucas makes 270 bookmarks per hour. After he works for 7 hours, how many bookmarks are left?

  1. 1890 bookmarks
  2. 2970 bookmarks ✔
  3. 4590 bookmarks
  4. 2430 bookmarks

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5

Done in 7 hours: 270 × 7 = 1890 bookmarks. Remaining: 4860 − 1890 = 2970 bookmarks.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 1890 is how many he already made (270×7), not how many are left. Subtract from the total: 4860 − 1890 = 2970.
C: 4590 looks like 4860 − 270 (only one hour). He worked 7 hours, so subtract 1890.
D: 2430 is half of 4860. Use rate × time first: 270×7=1890, then 4860−1890=2970.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Reporting done amount instead of remaining; subtracting only one hour of work.

Q3 · 求时间

小明每小时做 165 个零件，做完 1980 个零件要几小时？

  1. 10 小时
  2. 14 小时
  3. 12 小时 ✔
  4. 15 小时

Answer 答案: C · Level 難度 5

时间 = 工作量 ÷ 效率：1980 ÷ 165 = 12。所以要 12 小时。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 10 偏小。1980÷165：165×12=1980，恰好是 12 小时。
B: 14 偏大。可验算：165×12=1980，不是 14。
D: 15 像是用了别的除法。正确：1980÷165=12。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 估商偏大或偏小；用加减代替除法。

Q4 · Missing factor

What number belongs in the box? □ × 24 = 3552

  1. 138
  2. 148 ✔
  3. 158
  4. 146

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

Divide to check: 3552 ÷ 24. 24 × 100 = 2400, 3552 − 2400 = 1152; 24 × 48 = 1152, so 100 + 48 = 148. Check: 24 × 148 = 3552.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 24 × 138 = 3312, which is short of 3552 (short by 240). Try a larger quotient: 148.
C: 24 × 158 = 3792, which is past 3552. The quotient is too big; use 148.
D: 24 × 146 = 3504, only 48 short of 3552. One more step of 24 gives 148. (Yesterday □×18=2628 needed 146 — different problem.)

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Underestimating the quotient; stopping one step short when checking with multiplication.

Q5 · 算式对应问题

算式 (7200 − 2880) ÷ (2880 ÷ 8) 可以解答下面哪个问题？

  1. 李师傅 8 天做了 2880 个零件，照此速度做完 7200 个一共要几天？
  2. 李师傅 8 天做了 2880 个零件。已经做了 8 天，照此速度做完还剩的 7200−2880 个零件，还要几天？ ✔
  3. 李师傅每小时做 2880 个零件，做 8 小时一共做多少个？
  4. 7200 个零件，8 个人一起做，每人做多少个？

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5

先算效率：2880 ÷ 8 = 360（个/天）。再算剩余：7200 − 2880 = 4320。最后 4320 ÷ 360 = 12，就是「已做 8 天后，剩余还要几天」。算式结构正好是（总量−已做）÷（已做÷天数）。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 「一共要几天」对应的是 7200÷(2880÷8)=20，不是 (7200−2880)÷(2880÷8)。括号里先减再除，问的是剩余天数。
C: 那是乘法：2880×8，与这个除法算式无关。
D: 那是 7200÷8，没有用到 2880，也不符合算式结构。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 把「剩余天数」误当成「总天数」；忽略算式里的减法。

Q6 · Plan vs actual ⭐ Challenge 挑戰題

A factory planned to make 8640 toys in 12 days (same number each day). They finished all 8640 toys in only 8 days. How many MORE toys per day did they make than planned?

  1. 720 toys more per day
  2. 1080 toys more per day
  3. 360 toys more per day ✔
  4. 240 toys more per day

Answer 答案: C · Level 難度 5

Planned rate = 8640 ÷ 12 = 720 toys per day. Actual rate = 8640 ÷ 8 = 1080 toys per day. Difference = 1080 − 720 = 360 more toys per day.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 720 is the planned daily amount, not how many more than the plan. Subtract: 1080 − 720 = 360.
B: 1080 is the actual daily amount. The question asks how many more than planned: 1080 − 720 = 360.
D: 240 may come from (12−8)×60 or another mix-up. Find both daily rates first, then subtract.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Reporting plan or actual rate instead of the difference; using day difference without rates.
