Lesson 27 Work Rate + syllabus review (比较效率／多车单位／分配律×25／中括号) · Level 5 · Shanghai G4 / Singapore P4

Work ÷ Time = Rate (工作效率)
Rate × Time = Work (工作量)
Work ÷ Rate = Time (求时间／天数)
Unit rate from multi agents/trips: total ÷ trucks ÷ trips
×25 smart way: n×100÷4 or (a+b)×25
Order: ( ) then [ ], then ×÷, then +−

Q1 · 比较效率

小明 8 小时完成了 2880 个零件，王阿姨 6 小时完成了 2520 个零件。谁的工作效率更高？高出多少？

  1. 小明更高，每小时多 60 个
  2. 王阿姨更高，每小时多 60 个 ✔
  3. 王阿姨更高，每小时多 360 个
  4. 两人效率一样

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5

小明的效率：2880 ÷ 8 = 360（个/小时）。王阿姨的效率：2520 ÷ 6 = 420（个/小时）。420 − 360 = 60。所以王阿姨更高，每小时多 60 个。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 把两人效率搞反了。小明是 360，王阿姨是 420，应是王阿姨更高。
C: 360 是小明的效率，不是高出的部分。高出的是 420 − 360 = 60。
D: 360 ≠ 420，两人效率不一样。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 只比总量不比效率；把效率差值当成总量或某一边的效率。

Q2 · Multi-truck unit rate

5 trucks made 4 trips altogether and carried 10800 boxes of fruit. At this rate, how many boxes can 1 truck carry in 1 trip?

  1. 2160 boxes
  2. 2700 boxes
  3. 540 boxes ✔
  4. 1350 boxes

Answer 答案: C · Level 難度 5

First find one truck's total across all trips: 10800 ÷ 5 = 2160. Then one trip: 2160 ÷ 4 = 540. Or 10800 ÷ (5 × 4) = 10800 ÷ 20 = 540 boxes per truck per trip.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 2160 is 10800 ÷ 5 (one truck for all 4 trips). Divide by 4 more for one trip: 540.
B: 2700 is 10800 ÷ 4 (all trucks for one trip). Divide by 5 for one truck: 540.
D: 1350 may come from 10800 ÷ 8. Use both factors: 10800 ÷ 5 ÷ 4 = 540.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Dividing by only trucks or only trips; inventing another divisor.

Q3 · 求工作量

李师傅每小时修 145 个零件，他一共工作了 16 小时。李师傅一共修了多少个零件？

  1. 2320 个 ✔
  2. 1610 个
  3. 1450 个
  4. 2610 个

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 5

工作量 = 效率 × 时间：145 × 16。145 × 10 = 1450，145 × 6 = 870，1450 + 870 = 2320。所以一共修了 2320 个。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: 1610 像是 145×10+160 之类的误算。正确：145×16=2320。
C: 1450 只是 145×10，还少算了 6 小时：再加 870 得 2320。
D: 2610 偏大。验算：145×16=2320。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 只乘一部分时间；加减代替乘法。

Q4 · Distributive / ×25

Use a smart way to find 536 × 25. Which answer is correct?

  1. 5360
  2. 13400 ✔
  3. 1340
  4. 10720

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

Smart way: 536 × 25 = 536 × 100 ÷ 4 = 53600 ÷ 4 = 13400. Check: (500 + 36) × 25 = 12500 + 900 = 13400.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 5360 is 536 × 10. Remember ×25 = ×100 ÷ 4, not ×10.
C: 1340 is about 536 × 2.5 without placing zeros. Full product is 13400.
D: 10720 is 536 × 20. You still need another ×5: 536 × 5 = 2680, and 10720 + 2680 = 13400.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Treating ×25 as ×10; dropping a zero after ÷4; stopping at ×20.

Q5 · 中括号运算

计算：[840 ÷ (28 − 4)] × 36。正确结果是哪一个？

  1. 840
  2. 1050
  3. 1260 ✔
  4. 1440

Answer 答案: C · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

先算小括号：28 − 4 = 24。再算中括号里的除法：840 ÷ 24 = 35。最后 35 × 36 = 1260。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 840 是原题里的被除数，还没做完乘 36。35 × 36 = 1260。
B: 1050 可能来自 840÷24×30 或别的估乘。正确：35×36=1260。
D: 1440 像是 40×36。括号内是 24 不是 21，840÷24=35，不是 40。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 忘了最后一步乘法；括号算错导致商偏大。

Q6 · Head start then together ⭐ Challenge 挑戰題

There are 7200 books to pack. Emma packs 180 books per hour alone for 5 hours. Then Lucas joins her, and together they pack 420 books per hour. How many more hours do they need together to finish the rest?

  1. 12 hours
  2. 15 hours ✔
  3. 18 hours
  4. 10 hours

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5

Emma alone for 5 hours: 180 × 5 = 900 books. Remaining: 7200 − 900 = 6300 books. Together rate: 420 books per hour. Time left: 6300 ÷ 420 = 15 hours.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 12 ignores the head start or uses a wrong remaining. First subtract 900, then 6300 ÷ 420 = 15.
C: 18 may come from 7200 ÷ 400. Use remaining 6300 and rate 420: 15 hours.
D: 10 is 1800÷180 or similar mix-up. Done is 900, left is 6300, 6300÷420=15.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Forgetting to subtract Emma's head-start work; dividing the full total by the together rate.
