Lesson 27 Work Rate + syllabus review (分配律／括號／未知因數) · Level 4 · Shanghai G4 / Singapore P4

Work done ÷ Time = Rate (工作效率)
Rate × Time = Work done (工作量)
Remaining = Total − Rate × Time (剩餘工作量)
Distributive: (a±b)×c = a×c ± b×c (分配律)
Order: ( ) then [ ], then ×÷, then +− (運算順序)

Q1 · Remaining work

There are 2160 leaflets to fold. Chloe folds 180 leaflets per hour. After she works for 4 hours, how many leaflets are still left?

  1. 720 leaflets
  2. 1440 leaflets ✔
  3. 1980 leaflets
  4. 1080 leaflets

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 4 · Review 複習

Done in 4 hours: 180 × 4 = 720 leaflets. Remaining = 2160 − 720 = 1440 leaflets.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 720 is the amount already folded, not what is left. Remaining = 2160 − 720 = 1440.
C: You only subtracted one hour (2160 − 180). She worked 4 hours: subtract 720.
D: You may have halved 2160. Use remaining = total − rate × time = 2160 − 720 = 1440.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Reporting done instead of remaining; subtracting only one hour.

Q2 · Find the rate

Sophia sorted 1440 postcards in 8 hours. What is Sophia's work rate in postcards per hour?

  1. 180 postcards per hour ✔
  2. 11520 postcards per hour
  3. 1432 postcards per hour
  4. 1448 postcards per hour

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 4

Rate = work ÷ time = 1440 ÷ 8 = 180 postcards per hour. Check: 180 × 8 = 1440.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: You multiplied (1440 × 8). Rate is work divided by time.
C: You subtracted (1440 − 8). Use rate = work ÷ time.
D: You added (1440 + 8). Divide the postcards by the hours.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Multiplying, adding, or subtracting instead of dividing.

Q3 · Find the work done

Ethan builds 256 toy cars per hour. He works for 6 hours. How many toy cars does he build altogether?

  1. 262 toy cars
  2. 250 toy cars
  3. 1536 toy cars ✔
  4. 42 toy cars

Answer 答案: C · Level 難度 4

Work = rate × time = 256 × 6 = 1536 toy cars.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You added (256 + 6). Each hour he builds 256, so multiply.
B: You subtracted (256 − 6). Use work = rate × time.
D: You roughly divided. Use work = rate × time = 256 × 6.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Adding, subtracting, or dividing instead of multiplying.

Q4 · Distributive / make 100

Use a smart way to find 248 × 25. Which answer is correct?

  1. 2480
  2. 6200 ✔
  3. 6225
  4. 5200

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 4 · Review 複習

25 = 100 ÷ 4, so 248 × 25 = 248 × 100 ÷ 4 = 24800 ÷ 4 = 6200. Or (200 + 48) × 25 = 5000 + 1200 = 6200.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You may have done 248 × 10. Multiply by 25, not 10: 248 × 25 = 6200.
C: Close but off: 249 × 25 = 6225. Here the factor is 248, so 6200.
D: You may have used 200 × 25 + 40 × 5 or another slip. (200+48)×25 = 5000+1200 = 6200.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Treating ×25 as ×10; off-by-one on the factor; incomplete distributive split.

Q5 · Missing factor

What number belongs in the box? □ × 12 = 1452

  1. 120
  2. 121 ✔
  3. 122
  4. 17424

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 4 · Review 複習

Missing factor = 1452 ÷ 12. 12 × 120 = 1440; 1452 − 1440 = 12, so 12 × 1 more = 121. Check: 121 × 12 = 1452.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 12 × 120 = 1440, which is 12 short of 1452. The box is 121.
C: 12 × 122 = 1464, which is too big. Try 121.
D: You multiplied (1452 × 12). To find the missing factor, divide: 1452 ÷ 12.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Guessing nearby multiples; multiplying instead of dividing.

Q6 · Remaining work with brackets ⭐ Challenge 挑戰題

Noah must finish 2400 pages. He copies 160 pages per hour. He works for [3 + (5 − 2)] hours. How many pages are still left?

  1. 960 pages
  2. 1440 pages ✔
  3. 1920 pages
  4. 2240 pages

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5

Hours first: (5 − 2) = 3, then [3 + 3] = 6 hours. Done: 160 × 6 = 960 pages. Left: 2400 − 960 = 1440 pages.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 960 is the amount already copied, not what is left. Remaining = 2400 − 960 = 1440.
C: You may have used only 3 hours (160 × 3 = 480; 2400 − 480 = 1920). Evaluate the brackets: [3+(5−2)] = 6 hours.
D: You may have subtracted only one hour (2400 − 160). He worked 6 hours.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Skipping bracket evaluation; reporting done instead of remaining; using only part of the time expression.
