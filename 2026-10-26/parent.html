Lesson 27: Work Rate (工作效率／工程問題) · Level 2 · Shanghai G4 / Singapore P4

Work done ÷ Time = Rate (工作效率)
Rate × Time = Work done (工作量)
Work done ÷ Rate = Time (時間)
Work rate = the amount done in one unit of time (單位時間內完成的工作量)

Q1 · Find the rate

Oliver painted 24 fences in 4 hours. Noah painted 30 fences in 6 hours. Who works faster, and by how many fences per hour?

  1. Noah, by 6 fences per hour
  2. Oliver, by 1 fence per hour ✔
  3. Noah, by 1 fence per hour
  4. They work at the same rate

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 2

Rate = work ÷ time. Oliver: 24 ÷ 4 = 6 fences/h. Noah: 30 ÷ 6 = 5 fences/h. 6 − 5 = 1, so Oliver is faster by 1 fence per hour.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You compared the totals (30 − 24 = 6). You must first divide each total by its time to get the rate (工作效率).
C: You found the difference of 1 correctly but picked the wrong person: Oliver's rate (6) is higher than Noah's (5).
D: Check the division: 24 ÷ 4 = 6 and 30 ÷ 6 = 5 — they are not equal.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Comparing totals instead of rates; mixing up who is faster.

Q2 · Find the time

Mia packs 15 boxes per hour. How many hours does she need to pack 75 boxes?

  1. 60 hours
  2. 1125 hours
  3. 5 hours ✔
  4. 90 hours

Answer 答案: C · Level 難度 2

Time = work ÷ rate = 75 ÷ 15 = 5 hours.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You subtracted (75 − 15). To find time, divide the work by the rate.
B: You multiplied (75 × 15). Rate × time gives work, but here we already know the work — divide instead.
D: You added (75 + 15). Use time = work ÷ rate.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Multiplying, adding, or subtracting instead of dividing.

Q3 · Find the work done

Ava draws 9 pictures per hour. She draws for 4 hours. How many pictures does she draw altogether?

  1. 13 pictures
  2. 36 pictures ✔
  3. 5 pictures
  4. 2 pictures

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 2

Work = rate × time = 9 × 4 = 36 pictures.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You added (9 + 4). Each hour she draws 9, so it is 9 + 9 + 9 + 9 = 9 × 4.
C: You subtracted (9 − 4). Use work = rate × time.
D: You divided (9 ÷ 4). 9 is already the amount per hour; for 4 hours multiply.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Adding, subtracting, or dividing instead of multiplying.

Q4 · Remaining work

Mr. Smith can mow a lawn alone in 5 hours. After he works for 2 hours, what fraction of the lawn is still left?

  1. 2/5 of the lawn
  2. 3/5 of the lawn ✔
  3. 1/5 of the lawn
  4. 5/2 of the lawn

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 2

Treat the lawn as 1. Rate = 1 ÷ 5 = 1/5 per hour. In 2 hours he finishes 2 × 1/5 = 2/5. Left: 1 − 2/5 = 3/5.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 2/5 is the part already done, not what is left. Remaining = 1 − 2/5 = 3/5.
C: 1/5 is his rate per hour, not the remaining fraction after 2 hours.
D: You inverted 2 and 5. Rate = work ÷ time = 1 ÷ 5 = 1/5, then remaining = 3/5.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Reporting the done part instead of the remaining; confusing rate with remaining work.

Q5 · Find the rate

Noah baked 20 cookies in 4 hours. What is Noah's work rate in cookies per hour?

  1. 5 cookies per hour ✔
  2. 80 cookies per hour
  3. 16 cookies per hour
  4. 24 cookies per hour

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 2 · Review 複習

Rate = work ÷ time = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 cookies per hour.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: You multiplied (20 × 4). Rate is work divided by time, not multiplied.
C: You subtracted (20 − 4). Use rate = work ÷ time.
D: You added (20 + 4). Divide the cookies by the hours.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Multiplying, adding, or subtracting instead of dividing work by time.

Q6 · Working together ⭐ Challenge 挑戰題

Mia paints 6 walls per hour and Oliver paints 4 walls per hour. There are 30 walls. If they paint together, how many hours will it take?

  1. 5 hours
  2. 3 hours ✔
  3. 15 hours
  4. 10 hours

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 3

Together their rate is 6 + 4 = 10 walls per hour. Time = 30 ÷ 10 = 3 hours. Check: Mia 18 + Oliver 12 = 30.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You used only Mia's rate (30 ÷ 6). When working together, add the rates first.
C: You subtracted the rates (6 − 4 = 2, then 30 ÷ 2). Working together means adding rates.
D: 10 is their combined rate per hour, not the time. Next step: 30 ÷ 10.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Using one person's rate; subtracting rates; stopping at the combined rate.
