Rate × Time = Work；Remaining = Total − Done (剩余工作量)
Compare rates: find each person's work ÷ time, then subtract (比较效率)
Smart ×25: n×25 = n×100÷4 或 (a+b)×25 (分配律)
Missing factor: □ = product ÷ known factor (未知因数)
[ ] then ( ): brackets first (带中括号四则)

Q1 · 求未知因数

求未知因数：□ × 32 = 5984。方框里应填哪一个数？

  1. 167
  2. 187 ✔
  3. 177
  4. 197

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

未知因数 = 积 ÷ 已知因数：5984 ÷ 32。先估商：32 × 180 = 5760。5984 − 5760 = 224。224 ÷ 32 = 7。所以 180 + 7 = 187。检验：32 × 187 = 32 × 180 + 32 × 7 = 5760 + 224 = 5984。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 167 少估了 20。32 × 167 = 32 × 160 + 32 × 7 = 5120 + 224 = 5344，比 5984 小。正确是 5984 ÷ 32 = 187。
C: 177 少估了 10。32 × 177 = 32 × 180 − 32 × 3 = 5760 − 96 = 5664，比 5984 小。正确是 187。
D: 197 多估了 10。32 × 197 = 32 × 180 + 32 × 17 = 5760 + 544 = 6304，比 5984 大。正确是 187。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 估商偏低（少估 10 或 20）；未用乘法检验。

Q2 · Remaining work

There are 9360 name tags to stick on gifts. Emma sticks 384 name tags per hour. After she works for 11 hours, how many name tags are still left?

  1. 5136 name tags ✔
  2. 4224 name tags
  3. 8976 name tags
  4. 5520 name tags

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

Done = rate × time = 384 × 11. 384 × 10 = 3840 and 384 × 1 = 384, so 3840 + 384 = 4224 name tags done. Remaining = 9360 − 4224 = 5136 name tags.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: 4224 is how many Emma already stuck (384 × 11), not how many are left. Subtract from the total: 9360 − 4224 = 5136.
C: 8976 is 9360 − 384 (only one hour). She worked 11 hours: done = 384 × 11 = 4224, left = 5136.
D: 5520 is 9360 − 3840, which only subtracts 10 hours of work (384 × 10 = 3840). Include the 11th hour: 9360 − 4224 = 5136.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Reporting done instead of remaining; subtracting only one hour; forgetting the extra hour after multiplying by 10.

Q3 · 比较效率

小华 9 小时包装了 3510 个礼盒，赵叔叔 8 小时包装了 3280 个礼盒。谁的工作效率更高？每小时相差多少个？

  1. 小华更高，每小时多 20 个
  2. 赵叔叔更高，每小时多 20 个 ✔
  3. 两人效率一样
  4. 赵叔叔更高，每小时多 230 个

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

先求每人每小时的效率：小华 3510 ÷ 9 = 390（个/小时）；赵叔叔 3280 ÷ 8 = 410（个/小时）。410 − 390 = 20，所以赵叔叔效率更高，每小时多 20 个。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 小华每小时 390 个，比赵叔叔的 410 个少，不是更高。差确实是 20 个，但更高的是赵叔叔。
C: 两人效率不一样：390 ≠ 410。赵叔叔每小时多 20 个。
D: 230 是两人总量的差（3510 − 3280 = 230），不是每小时效率的差。应先各自除以时间：410 − 390 = 20。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 用总量比较而不是先求每小时效率；算对了差但弄反了谁更快。

Q4 · Distributive ×25

Use a smart method to calculate 672 × 25. Which result is correct?

  1. 6720
  2. 16800 ✔
  3. 67200
  4. 13440

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

Smart method: 672 × 25 = 672 × 100 ÷ 4 = 67200 ÷ 4 = 16800. Or use the distributive law: (600 + 72) × 25 = 15000 + 1800 = 16800.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 6720 is 672 × 10. ×25 is not ×10. Use ×100 ÷ 4: 67200 ÷ 4 = 16800.
C: 67200 is 672 × 100. You still need to ÷4 because 25 = 100 ÷ 4. 67200 ÷ 4 = 16800.
D: 13440 is 672 × 20. Still need another ×5, or use ×100 ÷ 4 → 16800.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Treating ×25 as ×10; multiplying by 100 and forgetting to ÷4; only multiplying by 20.

Q5 · 中括号四则

计算 [2160 ÷ (48 − 12)] × 56。正确结果是哪一个？

  1. 1848
  2. 2520
  3. 3360 ✔
  4. 2880

Answer 答案: C · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

先算小括号：48 − 12 = 36。再算中括号：2160 ÷ 36 = 60。最后：60 × 56 = 3360。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 1848 来自忘记先算小括号、写成 [2160÷48 − 12]×56 = (45−12)×56 = 33×56 = 1848。应先算 48−12=36，再 2160÷36=60，最后 60×56=3360。
B: 2520 是 [2160÷48]×56 = 45×56，忘记先减 12。应先 48−12=36，2160÷36=60，60×56=3360。
D: 2880 是 60×48，把最后要乘的 56 错用成了括号里的 48。正确是 60 × 56 = 3360。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 忘记先算小括号里的减法；运算顺序出错；最后一步用错乘数。

Q6 · Plan vs actual ⭐ Challenge 挑戰題

Mrs. Brown planned to pack 12600 juice boxes in 14 days. She actually finished all 12600 juice boxes in 9 days. How many more juice boxes did she pack per day than planned?

  1. 500 juice boxes more per day ✔
  2. 900 juice boxes more per day
  3. 1400 juice boxes more per day
  4. 2520 juice boxes more per day

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

Planned rate: 12600 ÷ 14 = 900 juice boxes per day. Actual rate: 12600 ÷ 9 = 1400 juice boxes per day. Difference: 1400 − 900 = 500 more juice boxes per day than planned.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: 900 is the planned daily rate, not how many more she packed. Actual is 1400, so more = 1400 − 900 = 500.
C: 1400 is the actual daily rate, not the difference. More = 1400 − 900 = 500.
D: 2520 comes from dividing the total by the days saved: 12600 ÷ (14 − 9) = 2520. That is not the extra per day. Compare the two rates: 1400 − 900 = 500.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Reporting the planned or actual rate instead of the difference; dividing the total by the days saved instead of comparing daily rates.
