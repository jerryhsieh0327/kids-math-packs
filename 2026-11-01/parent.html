Work ÷ Time = Rate (工作效率)
Rate × Time = Work；Remaining = Total − Done (剩余工作量)
Smart ×25: n×25 = n×100÷4 或 (a±b)×25 (分配律)
Same rate scale: days₂ = work₂ ÷ (work₁÷days₁)
Multi-agent unit rate: total ÷ trucks ÷ trips
Missing factor: □ = product ÷ known factor
[ ] then ( ): brackets first (带中括号四则)

Q1 · 分配律 ×25

用简便方法计算 448 × 25。正确结果是哪一个？

  1. 4480
  2. 11200 ✔
  3. 44800
  4. 8960

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

用分配律或“乘100再除4”：448 × 25 = 448 × 100 ÷ 4 = 44800 ÷ 4 = 11200。也可以写成 (400 + 48) × 25 = 10000 + 1200 = 11200。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 4480 是 448 × 10。×25 不是 ×10。正确算法：448 × 100 ÷ 4 = 11200。
C: 44800 是 448 × 100，还要再 ÷4（因为 25 = 100÷4）。44800 ÷ 4 = 11200。
D: 8960 像是 448 × 20。要用 ×25：448 × 25 = 11200。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 把 ×25 当成 ×10；乘了 100 忘记 ÷4；当成 ×20。

Q2 · Remaining work

There are 5670 stickers to put on cards. Emma puts on 315 stickers per hour. After she works for 8 hours, how many stickers are still left?

  1. 2520 stickers
  2. 3150 stickers ✔
  3. 5355 stickers
  4. 2880 stickers

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

Done = rate × time = 315 × 8. 315 × 8 = 2520 stickers. Remaining = 5670 − 2520 = 3150 stickers.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 2520 is how many she already put on, not how many are left. Subtract from the total: 5670 − 2520 = 3150.
C: 5355 looks like 5670 − 315 (only one hour). She worked 8 hours: done = 315 × 8 = 2520, left = 3150.
D: 2880 is not the remaining amount. Correct path: 315 × 8 = 2520 done, then 5670 − 2520 = 3150 left.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Reporting done instead of remaining; subtracting only one hour of work.

Q3 · 按效率推算天数

王阿姨 5 天缝了 1750 件衣服。照这样的速度，缝 2800 件衣服需要几天？

  1. 7 天
  2. 8 天 ✔
  3. 10 天
  4. 4 天

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

每天效率：1750 ÷ 5 = 350（件/天）。所需天数：2800 ÷ 350 = 8（天）。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 7 天不对。先求每天：1750÷5=350，再算 2800÷350=8。
C: 10 可能来自 2800÷280 或把 5 天直接比例做错。正确是 2800÷350=8。
D: 4 天少于已知的 5 天，但工作量更大，天数应更多。2800÷350=8。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 没先求每天效率；比例算反。

Q4 · Multi-truck unit rate

4 trucks made 3 trips altogether and carried 8640 boxes of fruit. At this rate, how many boxes can 1 truck carry in 1 trip?

  1. 2160 boxes
  2. 2880 boxes
  3. 720 boxes ✔
  4. 1080 boxes

Answer 答案: C · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

Divide by both the number of trucks and the number of trips: 8640 ÷ 4 = 2160, then 2160 ÷ 3 = 720. So 1 truck in 1 trip carries 720 boxes. (Or 8640 ÷ 3 ÷ 4 = 720.)

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 2160 only divides by the trucks (8640 ÷ 4). You still need to divide by the 3 trips.
B: 2880 only divides by the trips (8640 ÷ 3). You still need to divide by the 4 trucks.
D: 1080 is 8640 ÷ 8. Divide by trucks and trips separately: 8640 ÷ 4 ÷ 3 = 720.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Dividing by only trucks or only trips; inventing a combined divisor.

Q5 · 求未知因数

方框里应填什么数？□ × 18 = 2628

  1. 146 ✔
  2. 156
  3. 136
  4. 148

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

未知因数 = 积 ÷ 已知因数：2628 ÷ 18。18 × 140 = 2520，2628 − 2520 = 108，108 ÷ 18 = 6，所以 140 + 6 = 146。检验：18 × 146 = 2628。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: 156：18 × 156 = 2808，比 2628 大。正确是 2628 ÷ 18 = 146。
C: 136：18 × 136 = 2448，比 2628 小。正确是 146。
D: 148：18 × 148 = 2664，略大。2628 ÷ 18 = 146。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 除法估商偏差；未用乘法检验。

Q6 · Remaining after brackets + ×25 rate ⭐ Challenge 挑戰題

There are 9000 labels to stick. Oliver's rate is 48 × 25 labels per hour (use a smart way). He works for [3 + (6 − 2)] hours. How many labels are still left?

  1. 8400 labels
  2. 600 labels ✔
  3. 5400 labels
  4. 4200 labels

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

Rate: 48 × 25 = 48 × 100 ÷ 4 = 4800 ÷ 4 = 1200 labels per hour. Hours inside brackets: (6 − 2) = 4, then [3 + 4] = 7 hours. Done = 1200 × 7 = 8400. Remaining = 9000 − 8400 = 600 labels.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 8400 is how many he already stuck, not how many are left. Subtract: 9000 − 8400 = 600.
C: 5400 would match using only 3 hours (1200 × 3 = 3600 done → 5400 left). Do the full bracket time: [3+(6−2)] = 7 hours.
D: 4200 would match using only (6−2)=4 hours (1200 × 4 = 4800 done → 4200 left). Include the outer 3 as well: 7 hours total.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Reporting done instead of remaining; ignoring part of the bracketed time; wrong ×25.
