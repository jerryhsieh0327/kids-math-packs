Lesson 27 Work Rate + syllabus review (分配律×25／方位／剩餘) · Level 5 · Shanghai G4 / Singapore P4

Work done ÷ Time = Rate (工作效率)
Rate × Time = Work done (工作量)
Remaining = Total − Rate × Time (剩餘工作量)
×25 smart way: n×25 = n×100÷4 (簡便運算)
Directions: N/S/E/W and NE/NW/SE/SW (方位)

Q1 · Remaining work

There are 3840 bookmarks to make. Liam makes 240 bookmarks per hour. After he works for 5 hours, how many bookmarks are still left?

  1. 1200 bookmarks
  2. 2640 bookmarks ✔
  3. 3600 bookmarks
  4. 1920 bookmarks

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

Done in 5 hours: 240 × 5 = 1200 bookmarks. Remaining = 3840 − 1200 = 2640 bookmarks.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 1200 is the amount already made, not what is left. Remaining = 3840 − 1200 = 2640.
C: You only subtracted one hour (3840 − 240). He worked 5 hours: subtract 1200.
D: You may have halved 3840. Use remaining = total − rate × time = 3840 − 1200 = 2640.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Reporting done instead of remaining; subtracting only one hour.

Q2 · Find the time

Grace wraps 175 gifts per hour. How many hours does she need to wrap 1225 gifts?

  1. 7 hours ✔
  2. 214375 hours
  3. 1050 hours
  4. 6 hours

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 5

Time = work ÷ rate = 1225 ÷ 175. 175 × 7 = 1225, so she needs 7 hours.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: You multiplied (1225 × 175). Time is work divided by rate.
C: You subtracted (1225 − 175). Use time = work ÷ rate.
D: 175 × 6 = 1050, which is short of 1225. She needs 7 hours.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Multiplying or subtracting instead of dividing; off-by-one on the quotient.

Q3 · Find the work done

Owen packs 325 cartons per hour. He packs for 8 hours. How many cartons does he pack altogether?

  1. 333 cartons
  2. 2600 cartons ✔
  3. 317 cartons
  4. 40 cartons

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5

Work = rate × time = 325 × 8 = 2600 cartons.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You added (325 + 8). Each hour he packs 325, so multiply.
C: You subtracted (325 − 8). Use work = rate × time.
D: You roughly divided. Use work = rate × time = 325 × 8.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Adding, subtracting, or dividing instead of multiplying.

Q4 · Distributive / make 100 (×25)

Use a smart way to find 376 × 25. Which answer is correct?

  1. 3760
  2. 9400 ✔
  3. 9425
  4. 9000

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

25 = 100 ÷ 4, so 376 × 25 = 376 × 100 ÷ 4 = 37600 ÷ 4 = 9400. Or (400 − 24) × 25 = 10000 − 600 = 9400.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: You may have done 376 × 10. Multiply by 25, not 10: 376 × 25 = 9400.
C: Close but off: 377 × 25 = 9425. Here the factor is 376, so 9400.
D: Rough estimate only. Exact: 376 × 100 ÷ 4 = 9400.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Treating ×25 as ×10; off-by-one on the factor; approximating instead of exact.

Q5 · Directions

Emma walks from her house. The park is north of her house. The ice-cream shop is east of the park. In which direction is the ice-cream shop from Emma's house?

  1. north-east ✔
  2. south-east
  3. north-west
  4. east

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

From the house go north to the park, then east to the shop. Combined, the shop is north-east of Emma's house.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: South would be opposite of north. She goes north first, then east → north-east.
C: West is opposite of east. After going north, she goes east → north-east.
D: East alone ignores the north step. Both directions matter: north-east.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Using only one of the two directions; swapping north/south or east/west.

Q6 · Remaining work with ×25 rate ⭐ Challenge 挑戰題

There are 7200 labels to stick. Ella's rate is 36 × 25 labels per hour (use a smart way). After she works for 5 hours, how many labels are still left?

  1. 2700 labels ✔
  2. 4500 labels
  3. 180 labels
  4. 7175 labels

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 5

First find the rate: 36 × 25 = 36 × 100 ÷ 4 = 3600 ÷ 4 = 900 labels per hour. Done in 5 hours: 900 × 5 = 4500. Remaining = 7200 − 4500 = 2700 labels.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: 4500 is the amount already stuck, not what is left. Remaining = 7200 − 4500 = 2700.
C: You may have used 36 × 5 and ignored ×25. Rate is 900 per hour, not 36.
D: You only subtracted 25 (7200 − 25). Find rate first (900), then subtract 900 × 5.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Reporting done instead of remaining; skipping the ×25 rate step; subtracting the wrong quantity.
