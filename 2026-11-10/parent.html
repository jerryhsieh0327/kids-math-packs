Lesson 27 Work Rate + syllabus review (求效率／多车单位／推算天数／方位／结合律) · Level 5 · Shanghai G4 / Singapore P4

Work ÷ Time = Rate (工作效率)
Rate × Time = Work (工作量)
Work ÷ Rate = Time (求时间／天数)
Unit rate from multi agents: total ÷ (trucks × trips)
Directions: N/S/E/W + NE/NW/SE/SW (方位)
Associative: 125 × 8 = 1000; regroup to make 1000 (结合律)

Q1 · 求工作效率

下面是一张工作效率表。周阿姨工作了 18 小时，一共包装了 5292 个礼盒。表里「每小时包装个数」这一格是空的，应填多少？

  1. 294 个 ✔
  2. 304 个
  3. 284 个
  4. 5274 个

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 5

效率 = 工作量 ÷ 时间：5292 ÷ 18。18 × 290 = 5220，5292 − 5220 = 72，72 ÷ 18 = 4，所以 290 + 4 = 294。周阿姨每小时包装 294 个礼盒。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: 304 是估商偏大。18 × 300 = 5400，已经比 5292 多 108，应再减去 108 ÷ 18 = 6，得到 294，不是再往上加。
D: 5274 是 5292 − 18，用减法代替了除法。求每小时效率要用 5292 ÷ 18 = 294。
C: 284 是估商偏小。18 × 280 = 5040，5292 − 5040 = 252，252 ÷ 18 = 14，应是 280 + 14 = 294，不是 284。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 估商偏小或偏大；用减法代替除法。

Q2 · Multi-truck unit rate

8 trucks made 3 trips altogether and carried 13440 crates of apples. At this rate, how many crates can 1 truck carry in 1 trip?

  1. 1680 crates
  2. 560 crates ✔
  3. 4480 crates
  4. 5040 crates

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5

Total truck-trips: 8 × 3 = 24. Unit rate: 13440 ÷ 24 = 560 crates per truck per trip. (Or 13440 ÷ 8 = 1680 for one truck's 3 trips, then 1680 ÷ 3 = 560.)

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 1680 is 13440 ÷ 8 (one truck's 3 trips total), not one trip. Divide by 3 more: 560.
C: 4480 is 13440 ÷ 3 (all trucks in one trip). Still need ÷ 8: 560.
D: 5040 is 1680 × 3, multiplying instead of dividing by the 3 trips. Use ÷ (8 × 3) = ÷ 24 → 560.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Dividing by only trucks or only trips; multiplying instead of dividing by both.

Q3 · 按效率推算天数

吴师傅 12 天做了 4320 个零件。照这样的速度，做 7560 个零件需要几天？

  1. 21 天 ✔
  2. 16 天
  3. 18 天
  4. 24 天

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 5

先求每天效率：4320 ÷ 12 = 360 个。再求天数：7560 ÷ 360 = 21 天。也可以先求倍数：7560 ÷ 4320 = 7/4，12 × 7/4 = 21 天。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: 16 天没有按同样的效率放大。应用 4320 ÷ 12 = 360，再 7560 ÷ 360 = 21。
D: 24 天是把 12 天直接乘 2。7560 不是 4320 的 2 倍（2 倍是 8640），正确倍数是 7/4，12 × 7/4 = 21。
C: 18 天可能是效率算错后再除。正确效率是 4320 ÷ 12 = 360，7560 ÷ 360 = 21。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 没有先求出每天效率；把数量关系错看成整倍再乘天数。

Q4 · Directions

The museum is south of the school. The cafe is west of the museum. In which direction is the cafe from the school?

  1. south-east
  2. west
  3. south
  4. south-west ✔

Answer 答案: D · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

From the school, first go south to the museum, then from the museum go west to the cafe. South and then west together is south-west of the school.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: South-east would be south and then east. The cafe is west of the museum, not east.
B: West is only the second step. The cafe is also south of the school, so the direction is south-west.
C: South only reaches the museum. The cafe is still west of the museum, so from the school it is south-west.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Using only the first or only the second direction; mixing up east and west.

Q5 · 结合律简便运算

用简便方法计算 125 × 88 × 8。正确结果是哪一个？

  1. 11000
  2. 88000 ✔
  3. 80000
  4. 8800

Answer 答案: B · Level 難度 5 · Review 複習

先算 125 × 8 = 1000，再算 1000 × 88 = 88000。利用结合律把 125 和 8 结合成 1000，计算更简便。

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
A: 11000 是 125 × 88，漏乘了 8。完整应是 125 × 8 × 88 = 1000 × 88 = 88000。
C: 80000 是把 88 当成 80：125 × 8 × 80 = 80000。题目是 88，所以 1000 × 88 = 88000。
D: 8800 少写了一个 0。125 × 8 = 1000，1000 × 88 = 88000，不是 8800。

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: 漏乘 8；把 88 看成 80；结合成 1000 后少写一个 0。

Q6 · Head start then together ⭐ Challenge 挑戰題

There are 11520 books to pack. Oliver packs 240 books per hour alone for 8 hours. Then Mia joins him, and together they pack 640 books per hour. How many more hours do they need together to finish the rest?

  1. 15 hours ✔
  2. 12 hours
  3. 18 hours
  4. 48 hours

Answer 答案: A · Level 難度 5

Oliver alone for 8 hours: 240 × 8 = 1920 books. Remaining: 11520 − 1920 = 9600 books. Together rate: 640 books per hour. Time left: 9600 ÷ 640 = 15 hours.

Wrong options 錯誤選項:
B: 12 hours uses a wrong remaining or rate. Done is 1920, left is 9600, and 9600 ÷ 640 = 15.
D: 48 hours is 11520 ÷ 240, as if Oliver packed everything alone. After the head start, they work together at 640 books per hour for 15 hours.
C: 18 hours is 11520 ÷ 640, which ignores Oliver's 8-hour head start. First subtract 1920, then 9600 ÷ 640 = 15.

Common mistakes 常見錯誤: Forgetting to subtract Oliver's head-start work; dividing the full total by the together rate or by Oliver's rate alone.
